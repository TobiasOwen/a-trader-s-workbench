Same CSVs as the RSI teardown (Yahoo Finance daily, public chart API). Indicator-valid rows require SMA200, EMA200 and ATR(14) all defined. SMA verified vs a direct windowed mean; EMA (α=2/(n+1), seeded with SMA(n)) verified vs an independent ewm(adjust=False) implementation (agree <1e-9) and a hand-worked example — see `ma_lib.py`.

**Method.** Enter at the close of the signal bar (MA known at close; forming bar dropped; no look-ahead). Matched control = all same-asset days in the same trend regime (close vs 200d SMA) **and** the same ATR/close volatility tercile; edge vs that bucket mean. Effective n = distinct de-clustered episodes: price-vs-MA STATES are sampled throughout the state and de-clustered to ≥ horizon apart (not onset-only, because being above a MA is a persistent condition); crosses and support touches are onsets de-clustered to ≥ horizon apart so forward windows never overlap. Bootstrap p (10,000×) resamples each episode from its own matched bucket.

⚠ **Circularity (see §5a of the pre-registration).** The control conditions on the 200d-SMA regime, so **price vs SMA200 (state)** is a near-exact tautology — its up-state bars *are* the up-regime, so edge ≈ 0 by construction. The **golden/death cross (SMA 50/200)** and **price-cross-SMA200** are slow proxies of that regime (correlated, not identical). All three are re-run below against a trend reference independent of every tested MA (sign of the 120-day price change) to confirm the null is real, not manufactured by the control.
